What Miles Tracker is

Miles Tracker is a personal-finance tracking tool. It reads transaction-alert emails from your Gmail account and a Google Sheet you provide, and presents the results as spending summaries, bonus-cap progress, and portfolio figures. It runs entirely in your browser.

It is an independent hobby project. It is not a bank, a broker, a payment service or a licensed financial institution, and it is not affiliated with Google or with any bank, card issuer or fund whose data it displays.

The figures are estimates — always check them against your own statements

Miles Tracker's numbers are derived automatically and may be incomplete or wrong. Your bank, card issuer and broker are the authoritative record. Never rely on this app alone for a financial decision.

Specifically, and by design:

  • Email parsing is best-effort. Amounts, merchants, card numbers and dates are extracted from alert emails by pattern-matching. A change to a bank's email format, an alert that was never sent, or an unusual message can cause a transaction to be missed, duplicated or misread.
  • Bonus-cap progress is an approximation. Card categories, caps and qualifying-spend rules are configured by hand and may not reflect your issuer's current terms. Whether a transaction earns bonus miles is decided by your issuer, not by this app.
  • Foreign-currency figures may differ from what is charged. Alerts often carry estimated conversions; the final amount is set by your card network on settlement.
  • Reconciliation is automated matching and can be wrong. It may pair, correct, add or hide transactions incorrectly. You are asked to review its proposals for that reason.
  • Investment figures depend entirely on what you type into your sheet. Valuations, returns, MWRR and TWR are computed from your own entries and from prices in your sheet, which may be stale or wrong. They are not audited, not a statement of account, and not a performance record you should rely on.
